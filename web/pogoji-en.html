2. What Fixko is

Fixko is a listing platform that connects people who need help with work at home (“customers”) with registered businesses (“pros”). The customer posts a photo and a description of the job; pros of the matching trade in the customer's country and area see the post, and the pro the customer accepts calls the customer.

The provider is not a party to the contract between the customer and the pro, does not carry out any work, does not set prices and does not receive payments or commission for jobs. Price, timing, warranty and invoice are agreed directly between customer and pro.

3. For customers

  • Agree the price with the pro before the work starts and ask for an invoice. You can check the company and VAT number shown in the app in the business register of the pro's country or in the EU VIES system.
  • You can delete a post at any time.
