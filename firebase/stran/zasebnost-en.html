Privacy policy

Applies to the Fixko app (called Popravko in Slovenian). Effective from 27 October 2026.

1. Controller

The controller of personal data is REMAKO d.o.o., Ljubljanska cesta 63, 2327 Rače, Slovenia, company number 6569471000, VAT number SI45736901 (“we”). For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, write to support@remako.si.

We process data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Slovenian Personal Data Protection Act (ZVOP-2).

2. What data we process and why

Customers (people looking for a pro):

  • photo and description of the job, trade, town, area and country: to show the job to pros near you (performance of the service, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR);
  • phone number: so the pro who takes the job can call you (b);
  • notification token, app language and time zone: to tell you who is coming and when, in your language and local time (b).

Pros (businesses):

  • name, profile photo, company name and VAT or tax number: so the customer knows who is coming to their home and can check the company (b, and our legitimate interest in user safety, f);
  • phone, trades, country and areas of work, app language and time zone: to notify you of new jobs and connect you with the customer (b);
  • subscription status, if a subscription applies: Apple handles the payment entirely; we never see card details (b);
  • company verification: if you transfer €0.01 from the company account, we see the payer's name, account number and reference on our bank statement. If the company is reviewed instead, we check the VAT number in the EU VIES system (company name and address) or in a public business register. In the app we only store that the company is verified, the date and the check result (legitimate interest in preventing fraud, f);
  • the date you accepted the terms for pros (c and f).

All users:

  • a random anonymous device identifier (no password) that links your posts to your phone;
  • reports of inappropriate posts or pros: the reason, the job concerned and who reported it (f).

3. Location, voice and ads

In Slovenia, if you allow location, the app works out the nearest town on your phone. We never send or store your exact location; we only store the town name you see and can change. Location is optional. Outside Slovenia you type the town and choose the area yourself.

If you describe the job by voice (“Say it”), the recording is sent to Google Cloud Speech-to-Text (EU) for transcription. The recording is not stored; we only keep the text, which you can see and edit.

The app shows Google AdMob ads. Google uses the device advertising ID, device data and approximate location (from the IP address). Where required we ask for consent to personalised ads; you can change it in Settings › Ad settings. Without consent ads are not personalised. More: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Who sees your data

  • The photo, description, trade, town and time of a post are seen by pros of that trade in your country and area.
  • The customer's phone number is seen only by the pro the customer accepts.
  • When a pro takes a job, the customer sees their name, photo, company, VAT or tax number and phone.
  • Processors that store or transmit data for us: Google Ireland Ltd. (Firebase database, photo storage and server functions in the EU, Belgium region; speech-to-text; AdMob ads), Apple Distribution International Ltd. (notifications and subscriptions). We have data processing agreements with them. Any exceptional transfer outside the EU relies on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • We do not sell data and do not use it for profiling.

5. How long we keep it

  • Customer posts and photos: until you delete them, at most 12 months after the last change.
  • Pro profile: until you delete it or 24 months after last use.
  • Reports: 24 months, so we can act on repeated violations.
  • Data required by tax law: as long as the law requires.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. You can delete all your data immediately in the app: Settings › Delete my data. For anything else write to support@remako.si; we reply within one month.

You can complain to the Slovenian Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec RS, Dunajska cesta 22, 1000 Ljubljana, www.ip-rs.si) or to the data protection authority of your country.

7. Security

Data is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS). Database access is restricted by rules so that everyone sees only what belongs to them. Profile and job photos are accessible only to signed-in devices through unguessable links.

8. Children

Fixko is not intended for people under 15. Only businesses can register as pros.

9. Changes

We announce material changes to this policy in the app. The effective date is at the top.
